Load Historical Week

Fills weather fields from real Pantnagar records (2021/2022/2024) and gives you larval predictions from that year to test the model. All fields stay editable, so hypothetical future scenarios can be checked too. A result is only shown for weeks marked Validated in the week list below — weeks marked "Not validated" will show why no estimate is given instead.

Prototype — recommendations are decision support, not a substitute for field confirmation or local extension advice. Larval-density estimates come from regression fits to a small, Pantnagar-only dataset and can be wrong in any given week — always confirm with an in-field scouting count before acting.
